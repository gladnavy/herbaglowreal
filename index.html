Routine

Two steps, clearly explained.

Cleanse with shampoo, then condition according to your hair’s needs. Add oil or serum only if your hair asks for it.

1
Cleanse

Shampoo the scalp, not the lengths

Apply a generous amount to damp hair. Gently massage into the scalp, roots and ends, then rinse thoroughly.

2
Condition

Condition the lengths, not the roots

Squeeze out water. Apply conditioner to damp hair from mid-length to ends, leave it on for 2 minutes, then rinse thoroughly.

Twice a week, before washingMassage Herbal Hair Oil into the scalp and ends for 5–10 minutes. Leave 2–3 hours, then shampoo.
On damp or dry ends2–3 drops of Gold Oil on mid-lengths to ends. Avoid the scalp.

Which shampoo is right for me?

If your scalp gets oily within a day or builds up after 2 to 3 days, choose the Clarifying Herbal Shampoo. If it feels comfortable, dry or tight, choose the Herbal Shampoo. Not sure? Take the scalp quiz.

How soon will I see a difference?

Most customers mention softer hair after the first wash. Changes in hair fall take consistent use over several weeks. Results differ from person to person.

Can I use the oil and serum together?

Yes. Use Herbal Hair Oil twice a week before washing, and Hair Growth Serum daily on a clean scalp. Gold Oil goes on the ends only. If you have asthma, migraine or sinus sensitivity, don’t leave the hair oil on for more than 15 minutes.

Where do you deliver?

Across Malaysia. Standard delivery is RM 8 and express delivery is RM 15. Standard delivery is free on orders of RM 50 and above.

Which products are sulfate-free?

Our Herbal Shampoo, Clarifying Herbal Shampoo, Hi-Flex Conditioner, Hair Growth Serum, Gold Oil and Body Wash are all free from sulfates and parabens. Full ingredient lists are in the Ingredients section.
